# Privacy Policy — Krono Calendar

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This policy describes how the Android app **Krono Calendar** (package `com.edodm85.kronocalendar`), developed by edodm85, handles your information.

## In short

Krono has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. Your events stay on your device.

## Data that stays on your device

Everything you enter in Krono is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer:
- events, places, notes, colors, tags, reminders, urgent and completed events;
- the app settings.

**Device calendars.** If you tap "Connect calendars" and grant the permission, Krono reads and edits the events of the calendars already on your device (for example Google Calendar, CalDAV calendars through DAVx5, Exchange) through the Android system. Krono does not connect to these services directly: syncing with their servers is handled by their own apps and accounts, under their own privacy policies. You can choose which calendars to show and revoke the permission at any time in the Android settings.

**Files.** Backups (.json) and exports (.ics) are saved only to the files and folders you choose. The .ics files you open or import are read only to add their events.

**Android backup.** If device backup is turned on, Android may include Krono's database and settings in the backup of your Google account. The backup is managed by the system and the developer has no access to it.

## Donation (Google Play Billing)

The app offers an optional one-time donation through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: the developer never receives your payment details. The app only receives the result of the purchase, to show that you have already donated. The Google Play Billing library communicates with Google services and may send Google technical information about how it works. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Permissions

- **Calendar (read and write)**: to show, create and edit the events of the device calendars. Requested only when you connect your calendars.
- **Notifications, exact alarms, run at startup, vibration**: to deliver reminders at the exact time and restore them after the device restarts.
- **Internet, network state, billing**: used only by Google Play Billing for the donation.

## Children

The app is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your events in the app, clear the app data in the Android settings, or uninstall the app. Events in the device calendars stay in their calendars. The developer keeps no copies of your data.
